# Coordinated 3D quality pass

October 31, 2026, Asia/Manila. Implements the bundle proposed in RECONSTRUCTION_QUALITY_BRIEF.md. Code: `src/lib/quality/*`, `src/lib/services/quality.ts`, `src/lib/ai/quality.ts`, `src/components/scene/quality-controls.tsx`, route `/api/scenes/[sceneId]/quality`, quality steps in `src/workflows/generate.ts`.

## When it runs

New reconstructions run the free pass automatically right after ingest, replacing the old photo-texture copy (which stays as the fallback if the pass cannot run). Objects are settled on their supports first and the photo is baked onto that corrected pose, so the first saved result is the corrected one rather than a messy draft repaired later. Existing scenes use the button below.

## Two entry points

- **Check and repair (free).** `POST /quality {action:"IMPROVE"}` runs the deterministic pipeline on saved meshes, masks, camera and photo. No inference.
- **Use Meta AI (paid, confirmed).** `POST /runs {kind:"IMAGE_EDIT", confirmed:true, quality:{materials,masks,review}}` starts a durable run: high-reasoning Muse Spark plan per object → SAM 3.1 outline refinement where the plan shows a mask error (≤3 objects) → Muse Image material samples (≤6 per object, ≤60 total) → the same deterministic pipeline using those results → Muse Spark review of the final renders. Each provider call is one step with `maxRetries=0`; a failed call is recorded as uncertain and never resubmitted. Plans and samples are saved (`settings.qualityPlan`, private assets) and reused for the same source mesh and mask.

SAM 3D Objects stays the reconstruction model. The quality pass never re-meshes; a different reconstruction still goes through the existing selective retry.

## Pipeline order (`runQualityPipeline`)

1. **Diagnose.** `diagnoseMesh` reports per layer: geometry (degenerate/duplicate faces, components and fragment area, short boundary loops as tear candidates vs long ones as possible real openings, non-manifold edges, winding conflicts, folds, flipped stored normals), mapping (UV presence, degenerate/flipped/out-of-range UVs, island overlap, atlas occupancy, texel-density spread), lighting (base-color luminance correlated with facing-up normals) and material (texture roles, alpha, transforms). Private inspection views: wireframe, normals, unlit base color and neutral light from front/right/back/left/top/underside, plus the reference-camera composite.
2. **Geometry.** `repairGeometry` removes degenerate and duplicate faces, removes small fragments only when the reviewed mask does not cover them (disconnected legs, laces and handles stay), and fixes winding (open shells keep their majority orientation, closed shells face outward). A Spark `planar` constraint flattens only visible, already-flat faces in its evidence box: low residual relative to the patch size, interior vertices only, ≤2.5% displacement. Curves, slants, deformable and organic subjects are never flattened. UVs are untouched. `compareGeometry` accepts the candidate only when structure improves and surface area, extent, UVs and source-camera silhouette do not regress.
3. **Placement.** `solvePlacement` works in support order on the accepted meshes. It seats on the highest of up to 16 real base contacts (the floor, the support mesh's upward surface, or the level desk/table plane when that surface is background). It then lays the object flush: the smallest tilt (≤15°) that gives near-maximal flat base contact with the center of mass over it. Flat poses are re-fit to the photo (sliding on the support, heading, relative scale, every candidate scored while resting) instead of being compared with the unconstrained tilted fit. The photo can veto a flush pose only within 0.02 overlap, or 0.06 when the current pose cannot physically stand. Round or steep bases are left alone. It also and pushes geometry out of observed walls while letting it pass through windows/openings. It then separates intersecting neighbors. Dependents move with their supports. The layout is kept only if physical problems drop and no object's photo overlap drops >0.03; otherwise the regressed objects are held and the rest retried once. Unknown support, wall-mounted and suspended objects are not snapped to the floor. A tabletop item without a support mesh now keeps its estimated plane instead of dropping to the floor (also fixed in `reviewLayout`).
4. **Bake.** `bakeMaterials` on the accepted mesh at its final pose, with one shared depth/owner buffer. Per texel, inside validated UVs only: **observed** = aligned, de-lit source pixels on camera-facing surfaces, behind depth (scaled to object size, so thin walls do not swap pixels), owner, eroded-mask and backdrop checks, and feathered away from rims and silhouettes where small misalignment lands pixels on the wrong surface; **protected** Spark regions (logos, text, numbers, stitching) copy source pixels at full weight; **generated** = Muse samples through grain-aligned triplanar coordinates, color-matched to the observed region; **inferred** = provider atlas, harmonized by a bounded per-region gain. Material regions come from observed or provider color, smoothed over mesh adjacency. De-lighting divides low-frequency shading within chroma/lightness clusters and drops clipped highlights; it is an estimate. Only base-color images change. Data maps, alpha, sRGB and orientation are preserved. An explicit Meta material pass can bake 2K/4K base-color atlases; the free pass retains existing dimensions. Paired UV borders of the same material are reconciled inside their triangles, followed by resolution-scaled padding into unused texels. Coverage is area-weighted over mesh triangles; seams are measured across UV seam edges.
5. **Review.** Renders the final scene from the reference camera and compares it with the previous models at the same pose: silhouette overlap, occlusion, color error vs the de-lit photo, coverage, seams, contacts, stability, walls, collisions and possible duplicates. One bounded correction: an object whose own result regressed keeps its previous model. Spark proposals (`reseat`, `separate`, `wall-clearance`, `revert-object`) apply only when a numeric check supports them.

## Storage, guards and restore

New models are `MODEL_GLB` assets with provenance (`coordinated-quality-v1`, source asset, mask, geometry/UV hashes, coverage). `settings.quality` keeps the report and a backup of the previous model/mask/transform per object, appearance and layout review. Writes use scene revision CAS and per-object `modelAssetId` CAS. Repeated passes start from the same pre-bake source, so bakes never stack. **Restore previous models** puts back models, masks, transforms, photo-bake state and layout review; an object retried since keeps its newer model. While a pass is applied, the old photo-texture copy is blocked with a clear message; photographed floor/wall surfaces stay. Publication freezes the baked GLB like any other model, so textures follow move, rotate, save/reload and publish.

- Placement: a chair floating 1.3 cm sat down and its overlap rose 0.759→0.797; four wobbling bases (side table, dining chair, nightstand, armchair) now rest on 4–5 contacts; a vase 39 cm above the floor and a stone 51 cm above it settled; a stone/pebble intersection was removed. - SAM 3D meshes in these fixtures were topologically clean (no winding conflicts, almost no degenerate faces), so geometry repair rarely triggered. The chair's wavy seat edge is visible in normals/wireframe but is a shape error that the conservative repair does not straighten.

### Reported desk scene (a26c2b0b), offline from saved data

The saved layout kept SAM 3D pose errors: bases tilted against the level desk by 4.3° (laptop), 11.6° (mug), 3.0° (mouse) and 8.7° (clock), each touching at one point. The saved textures came from the old photo copy onto those tilted poses with a silhouette shift, which put the mug's rim inside the cup, its inside-wall shadow on the outside, and shifted the keys. After the pass every object rests flat with 8–16 contacts, and photo overlap improved for all four (laptop 0.905→0.960, mug 0.785→0.840, mouse 0.871→0.995, clock 0.922→0.931). The keys and "10:24" come from the photo on the corrected pose, and the mug's rim and inside-wall smears are gone. The same rule improved other fixtures (armchair 0.667→0.897, dining chair 0.759→0.827) with no new intersections. Nothing was written to that scene.

## Limitations

- November 1: a live four-object Spark/Muse material pass ran on 22632152 with five saved material samples and final Spark review. The first bake exposed shadow-based material misclassification and loss of texture-only structural detail; see the fidelity revision below. SAM outline refinement was not requested in that run. Native browser verification remains unavailable.
- Only `planar` constraints change geometry. Straight edges, symmetry and wavy silhouette edges (the chair seat) are reported, not repaired. Holes are never filled; tears are only reported.
- Coverage of unseen faces is still mostly provider appearance unless the paid pass adds Muse samples. Hidden appearance is plausible, not recovered.
- De-lighting is a local estimate. Strong cast shadows across one material can remain, and the color-error metric is measured against that same estimate.
- Contact stability uses sampled vertices and a flat-support assumption for tilt search. Upright correction beyond 5° and support-normal alignment on sloped supports are not attempted. Occupancy checks are approximate for thin/open geometry.
- Texture transforms, compressed primitives and meshes without UVs keep their original appearance (reported, not unwrapped).
- The Inspect links and quality panel have not been checked in a browser.
- A white mug still shows faint banding where photo and provider texels meet, and the white mouse's color error rose slightly (70.9→74.4). Both stay within the revert threshold.
- Flush alignment assumes a level support plane for background desks and floors; sloped supports keep their pose.

## Reconstruction fidelity revision — November 1

Textures now offers persisted Undo/Redo (eight changes), 2K/4K atlas size, photo-detail blend, explicit fresh material analysis/generation, optional outline checking, background rebuilding and fresh environment reanalysis. Quality calls run in separate durable steps per object/material, with a 90-minute run deadline. Existing samples are reused unless refresh is explicitly requested. Local diagnostic/overlay preparation happens before recording a paid attempt. A source-sized magenta overlay is composited before resizing; this fixes the reported Sharp failure on 1600×900 input.

Material labels use shading-tolerant chroma plus Spark's source-coordinate material evidence. Calibration uses masked material crops even for thin parts excluded from detail transfer. Muse supplies new intrinsic material detail; a bounded provider high-frequency signal retains joinery/folds/control features. Full-weight protected source markings remain separate. Grain-aligned baking covers supported unseen UVs, paired same-material UV borders are reconciled, and atlas padding grows with resolution. Shared base-color/data-role images are not resized. The viewer uses device-bounded anisotropic filtering on owned base-color texture copies.

The 4K option controls the **baked atlas**, not a promise of native 4K Muse generation. [Meta's image guide](https://dev.meta.ai/docs/image-generation) states that requested size controls aspect ratio, not exact resolution. Samples retain actual returned detail without enlargement; a larger atlas increases bake density, not missing photographic information. [Hosted SAM 3D Objects](https://fal.ai/models/fal-ai/sam-3/3d-objects/api) has no documented mesh-resolution/topology knob. No invented parameter or replacement provider is used.

`settings.appearanceHistory` stores bounded private before/after model, mask, transform, stage and render-setting snapshots. Apply, restore, rebuild and variant selection record a version in the same transaction. Final review coalesces into the same material operation. Undo/redo is guarded by owner, expected revision, active runs, photo/current-state fingerprint and same-scene READY assets; later transform/model edits must be undone first. Product fields, color, visibility and lighting remain independent. Analysis caches do not invalidate texture history. History never enters frozen public snapshots or triggers inference.

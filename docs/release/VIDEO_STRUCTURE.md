# ReScene demo video structure

Planning only, November2. This is a shot/timing outline, not narration or a script. Target2:55, leaving five seconds below the organizer's3:00 maximum. Use understandable English, readable captions and the working app/code. No slide deck or future-feature segment.

## Central story

Follow a product scene from seller creation to shopper decisions. The differentiator is the complete path from a photo to an editable environment and then a constrained purchasing decision, not a tour of every editor control. The budget stylist is the main payoff: keep a product, request a coordinated look, inspect two affordable alternatives in context and apply one.

Jim confirmed that no ReScene product existed before the event. The repository was created October28. Opening coverage should establish that baseline and the concrete event-built functionality while showing the app, without drafting a spoken line yet.

These durations are editorial allocations, not model-latency promises. If anything must be shortened, reduce secondary album/publish clicks before reducing the two-option stylist payoff or evidence that real generation happened. Do not accelerate speech to fit.

## Recording choices

Choose one simple, high-quality furniture scene with distinct silhouettes and visible cloth/wood materials, and a budget scenario that yields two meaningfully different available combinations. Rehearse the current72-product catalog: do not assume earlier quotes/layouts remain the best choices. Reading Corner is the first stylist candidate; Home essentials is an alternative if its refrigerator is marked owned. Quiet workspace with owned laptop/notebook, kept tumbler and$100 budget is a verified backup, but furniture is the stronger main visual story. Mention broader product support only through a quick actual example if time remains.

Record at1080p in desktop mode with a consistent theme and browser framing. Close private tabs/notifications, crop nothing essential, and keep product/price/response labels legible. Use the app's existing reference comparison and normal controls. Keep the original uncut capture, then make clearly marked cuts around genuine waits. A recording of real calls can be edited; it must not masquerade as a new live request when replaying saved samples. Do not generate another scene merely because the edit changed.

The video should visibly exercise Muse Image, Spark, SAM3.1 and SAM3D. Add Voice Transcribe through the stylist if its microphone path is rehearsed. VGGT is background depth evidence and should be attributed even if its full processing screen is not shown. The six-model inventory belongs in the repository; trying to explain every endpoint verbally would consume the demo.

## Proof and handoff

Keep a small evidence index linking the displayed input, generated image, approved output hashes and sanitized model/stage records to repository files. Separate newly recorded calls, prepared catalog artifacts and deterministic graphics/code. Publish no raw private receipts or access credentials. Keep real merchant/product claims out of the demo when the catalog fields are authored demonstration content.

Before recording: finish the repository/judge-setup work, resolve runtime model/asset rights questions, choose the scene and validate a fresh stylist request once with explicit recording intent. No paid requests are needed for planning. Record one full take, edit to2:55, confirm English readability/audio and timestamps, upload to an accessible video host, and test the link signed-out. Submission is a later explicit action.

Acceptance: a new viewer can explain the problem, identify what was built during the event, see actual Meta-powered generation and describe the shopper outcome without reading the README. The video contains no unbuilt feature, fake live inference, simulated checkout charge, unsupported accuracy metric or unlicensed asset.
